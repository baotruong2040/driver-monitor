# RESET/UVC investigation, 2026-10-06

Before diagnosis, Windows exposed COM11 (303A:1001) but no UVC CAM1.
USB reset allowed esptool to communicate. Watchdog reset alone did not restore UVC.
The app matched the built image at 0x10000; bootloader and partition table also
matched the local files. No partition or eFuse change was required.

A temporary diagnostic app used USB Serial/JTAG console output and waited five
seconds before camera startup. Captured logs confirmed OV3660, available 8 MB
PSRAM, HD 1280x720, JPEG quality 10, XCLK 20 MHz, and four valid JPEGs.
Windows then enumerated UVC CAM1 (303A:8000); COM11 disappeared as expected.
The ten-second receiver check obtained 131 frames, 13.42 FPS, zero decoder-error
lines and a maximum frame gap of 232.4 ms. USB diagnostic logging makes this
unsuitable as the normal streaming performance baseline.

The precise original failure has not been isolated by an A/B test. These results
confirm that the sensor, capture path, USB link and host UVC driver can work.

The normal app restores UART0 logging, keeps the five-second startup
wait and explicitly disconnects/reconnects TinyUSB with a 250 ms detach interval.
PlatformIO now requests watchdog_reset after upload. The camera source's quality
10 setting is preserved. Build and host startup/clock checks passed. PlatformIO
uploaded the app, bootloader and partition table and performed watchdog reset.
Windows enumerated UVC CAM1 after the startup wait. A 30-second normal receiver
test obtained 517 frames at 17.43 FPS, zero decoder-error lines, and a maximum
frame gap of 82.2 ms. Physical RESET/power-cycle repetition is still pending.

Original source/config and firmware snapshots are under
`.pio/reset-diagnostic-backup/`; raw captured logs are `.pio/reset-startup.log`.
